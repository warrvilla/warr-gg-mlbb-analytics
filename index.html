Questions

The things people ask first

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope, so we quote per job rather than publish a price list. As a rough guide, a standalone brand identity or a small site usually starts in the tens of thousands of pesos; a full identity plus website sits higher. You get the number in writing before anything begins, and it does not move unless you change the scope.

How long does it take?

A focused sprint is two weeks. A brand identity is typically three to five weeks. A brand plus website is usually six to ten. We give you the date at the brief stage and we hold it — if something is going to slip, you hear it from us early, not on the deadline.

Do you work with clients outside the Philippines?

Yes. We are on GMT+8 and work with teams across Southeast Asia and beyond. Most of the work happens asynchronously, with calls scheduled around your timezone rather than ours.

What do we need to prepare before starting?

Less than you think. A sense of what you do, who it is for, and a few references of work you like — including work you dislike, which is often more useful. If you have existing assets, logos or copy, send them. If you do not, that is fine; we build from zero regularly.

How many revisions are included?

Two full rounds are built into every scope as standard. In practice, agreeing the direction before production means most projects do not use both. Extra rounds beyond that are quoted separately so the price stays honest rather than padded upfront.

Do we own the files afterwards?

Yes. You get source files, editable systems and plain-language guides at handover. You own what you paid for and you can run it, edit it, or hand it to another studio without needing us.
